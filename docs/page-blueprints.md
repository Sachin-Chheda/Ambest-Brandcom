# Page blueprints implemented

## Shared system

Every page uses server-rendered HTML, skip navigation, a real parent link plus toggle for desktop dropdowns, touch-friendly mobile disclosure groups, breadcrumbs on nested routes, a single canonical origin, private-preview noindex controls, organization/breadcrumb structured data, contextual quote actions and the shared footer.

## Home and company

- Home: global-partner hero, five primary services, India/APAC experience, global-company collaboration model, buyer situations, selected work, connected delivery narrative, useful articles, buyer questions and quote invitation.
- About: documented origin/name story, capability evolution, operational principles, responsibility types, media-permission boundary and published-contact summary.
- Contact: exact published contact blocks, project versus privacy routes and no invented hours/SLA.

## Divisions

The six primary commercial services are Ad Films & Video Content, Brand Communication & Strategy, Creative Solutions, Digital & Social, Website Development, and Brand Experiences & Partnerships. SEO, video-production and digital-marketing routes remain as deeper capability and evidence paths rather than the top-level positioning.

- Overview: buyer fit, distinct pathways/services, scope, measurement, boundaries, questions and quote route.
- How It Works: four offer records, stable IDs, distinct fit/objective/work/inputs/boundaries/measures, stages and responsibilities.
- Solution/service: unique problem modules, workstreams, dependencies, limits, evidence treatment, questions and preselected quote.
- Results: shared project records filtered by actual service relationship; qualitative cards do not imitate incomplete metric cards.

## Work and editorial

- Work: one canonical project page per engagement, one collection reused across division indexes.
- Project: header, scope, starting point, editorial interpretation, actual work, cautious outcomes, evidence boundary and next step.
- Blog: three meaningful categories, real dates and organization byline, no fake subscription.
- Article: breadcrumb, date/byline, opening answer, practical points, commissioning guidance, related content and service action.

## Quote

The direct page accepts proportionate required fields, optional context, allowlisted preselection and real server responses. Production success is impossible until the approved Turnstile keys and HTTPS lead destination are configured.
