# SEO and AI-search plan

## Foundations

- Keep meaningful content, navigation and links in initial HTML.
- Use one approved canonical host and direct permanent redirects.
- Include only successful, canonical, indexable routes in the public sitemap.
- Return a real 404 and avoid SPA fallback.
- Keep private review deployments noindex and excluded from the sitemap.
- Preserve real Mumbai relevance without scaled city or country variants.

## Intent boundaries

Overview pages support agency/service evaluation. How It Works pages support engagement choice. Solution pages answer specific buying questions. Results indexes expose evidence. Cases explain actual scopes. Articles answer informational questions. The site does not create a separate page for every keyword variant.

## Structured data

Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList and BlogPosting data describe visible facts. Service-specific data can be added after final commercial scope approval. Do not add aggregate ratings, awards, prices, unconfirmed locations, staff or “AI schema.”

## AI-assisted search

Useful identity, explicit scope, clear methods, attributable project evidence and accessible media are the implementation priorities. They do not guarantee inclusion or citation. Review Google generative-search controls/reports and OpenAI OAI-SearchBot/GPTBot choices at launch using current official documentation. Preserve the owner's independent training preference.

## Post-launch observation

Record engine/report, date, query or page, market/context, observation and limits. Do not publish a synthetic “AI visibility score” or treat a handful of manual prompts as representative.
